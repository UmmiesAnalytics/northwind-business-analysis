# Northwind Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses the Northwind database to analyse business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate product and customer activity, and generate actionable recommendations.

Using Power BI, DAX, Power Query and data modelling, I developed an interactive business intelligence solution designed to help decision-makers understand performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Business Problem

Businesses need reliable reporting to understand what drives revenue, which products perform well, where customers are located and how operational performance can be improved.

This project explores Northwind's historical business data to answer these questions and translate the findings into practical business recommendations.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products and categories.
- Analyse customer purchasing activity and geographic distribution.
- Examine sales trends across different periods.
- Evaluate employee performance.
- Develop KPIs and interactive dashboards.
- Translate analytical findings into business recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Microsoft Excel
- SQLite
- Data Modelling
- Data Visualisation

## Project Workflow

1. **Data preparation:** Reviewed the source tables, corrected data types, addressed data quality issues and prepared the dataset for analysis.
2. **Data modelling:** Established relationships between relevant tables to support accurate reporting.
3. **DAX development:** Created measures for sales, orders, average order value, quantity sold, customers, products and freight.
4. **Business analysis:** Investigated sales, products, customers, geographic performance and employee performance.
5. **Dashboard development:** Built interactive Power BI dashboards with KPIs, charts and filters.
6. **Business recommendations:** Converted findings into practical recommendations for management.

## Key Findings

The analysis produced the following findings:

### 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total revenue reached approximately **$1.35 million**.
- The business recorded **830 orders** across **93 customers**.
- A total of **77 products** contributed to the sales activity.
- Approximately **51,000 units** were sold.

### 2. Sales Performance
- April 1998 recorded the highest monthly sales, reaching **$134,630.56**.
- The 1997 reporting period generated approximately **$658,390** in sales.
- The 1998 reporting period generated approximately **$469,770**, covering only January to May.

### 3. Product Performance
- Beverages was the leading product category by sales.
- Category-level performance provides a basis for evaluating product contribution and identifying opportunities for growth.

### 4. Geographic Performance
- The United States ranked first among the five leading sales countries.
- Geographic sales analysis helps identify important markets and potential expansion opportunities.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Strengthen high-performing product categories:** Review the factors driving beverage sales and use those insights to guide inventory and product planning.
2. **Improve sales forecasting:** Use historical monthly sales trends to support demand planning and revenue forecasting.
3. **Prioritise key markets:** Maintain strong customer relationships in leading markets while evaluating opportunities in other geographic regions.
4. **Monitor employee performance:** Use employee-level sales indicators to identify training needs and opportunities to improve sales effectiveness.
5. **Improve performance monitoring:** Track revenue, order volume, average order value and product contribution through regular KPI reporting.

*Note: These recommendations are based on the patterns identified in the historical Northwind dataset. They are analytical recommendations, not evidence of actions already implemented.*
