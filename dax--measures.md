Northwind DAX Measures and Business 
Metrics 
1.	Overview 
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) was used in Power BI to create measures for calculating key business performance indicators. These measures support the Northwind sales analysis and power the dashboard's KPI cards, charts, and other visuals. 
2.	Measures Created 
Total Sales 
Purpose: Calculates the total sales value from the Order Details table. 
Business use: Provides an overall view of sales performance and supports comparisons across products, categories, countries, and time periods. 
 
Total Orders 
Purpose: Counts distinct order IDs. 
Business use: Measures order volume and helps identify changes in customer purchasing activity. 
 
 
Average Order Value 
Purpose: Calculates average sales value per order. 
Business use: Helps assess the average value generated by each order and supports comparisons across reporting periods. 
 
Total Quantity Sold 
Purpose: Calculates the total quantity of products ordered. 
Business use: Helps assess product demand and compare sales volume across products and categories. 
 
Total Customers 
Purpose: Counts distinct customer IDs. 
Business use: Provides a view of the customers represented in the current reporting context. 
 
Total Products 
Purpose: Counts distinct product IDs. 
Business use: Indicates the number of products represented in the product catalogue and supports product-level analysis. 
 
Total Freight 
Purpose: Calculates the total freight charges recorded in the Orders table. 
Business use: Supports analysis of shipping-related costs recorded against orders. 
 
3.	Filter Context and Interactivity 
Power BI measures respond to the filters and slicers applied to a report, provided the model relationships allow those filters to propagate correctly. 
For example, selecting a year or country can change the displayed sales, order count, and average order value. This allows users to investigate business performance across different segments. 
4.	Business Applications 
Together, these measures support analysis of: 
●	Overall sales performance. 
●	Order volume and average order value. 
●	Product demand and category performance. 
●	Customer counts. 
●	Product catalogue size. 
●	Recorded freight charges. 
●	Performance across dates and geographical markets. 
5.	Important Calculation Notes 
Total Sales accounts for the discount recorded on each order-detail line. 
Average Order Value is calculated by dividing Total Sales by Total Orders. Its interpretation depends on the sales definition and the current report filters. 
Total Freight represents the freight amounts recorded in the Orders table. It should not automatically be interpreted as the company's complete shipping cost or profit impact.
